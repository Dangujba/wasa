Development Privacy Notice

WASA pre-alpha account system. Effective 12 October 2026.

This notice describes the information used by the WASA development website at wasa.kasua.ng. WASA is currently a development project, not a released multiplayer game.

What we collect

When you register, the system stores your chosen username, email address, a securely derived password hash, registration time, and a player profile (display name, home city, level, XP, and your chosen avatar appearance such as outfits, caps, hairstyle, and colours). Your browser receives a secure session cookie. Security systems may process your IP address temporarily to limit abusive requests. We do not currently collect payment details or operate voice chat.

How we use your information

Account information is used to sign you in, remember your player profile, maintain development sessions, and protect the website from abuse. Player data is not currently used for advertising or sold to advertisers.

Security and retention

Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are stored as salted, computationally derived hashes, not plain text. Login session records expire after 14 days; expired records may remain until routine cleanup. Account records are held while your development account exists.

Development limitations

Email verification and automated password recovery are not available in this release. Do not reuse an important password. The world, clothing shop, racing, spectator system, and currency are not yet operational.

Your choices

You may update your display name, sign out, or permanently delete your development account through the account panel. Deletion removes the stored account, associated profile, and sessions from the live database. Standard operational backups, if retained, may remain until their retention period expires.

Updates

This notice will be revised before wider public testing and whenever major data processing features are introduced. Do not submit sensitive personal information to WASA during pre-alpha development.
